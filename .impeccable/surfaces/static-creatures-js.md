# Creatures

Mode: Operate

Audience: Bibin, teaching a creature while the others stay on the page. Task: circle one and set what it reads, when it wakes, the steps, and what it leaves. Constraints: those facts stay in view, plain speech stays, the top bar stays Paper Workbench.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The creatures page is a printed catalog of stamps, and the one you are teaching is circled in red. It refuses the dark wandering pen and the three-column bench.

OWN-WORLD: The Library desk. Graphite #17181b, sheets #222326, yellow #ffe347 for the circle, the path, and pressed controls. Instrument Serif for the page title, Doto for creature names, Geist for the controls.

STORY: Every creature is a stamp in the grid. Circling one teaches it, along a red line, without the others leaving. You still hatch, save, run, and talk to Dot in the same words.

FIRST VIEWPORT: CREATURES in condensed caps across the top. The stamp grid fills the left. The right plate is the circled creature's path: Reads, the step, Leaves you. If none is circled, the plate is Dot. The grid is in the first screen.

FORM: A variation of the convention circle catalog. Stamp grid, pen circle, red route. Not the hall, the zones, or its headline. seed:9c3d465d04d74d07
